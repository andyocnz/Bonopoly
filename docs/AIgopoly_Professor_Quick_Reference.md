# AIgopoly: Professor Quick Reference

**AI-Powered Business Simulation for Teaching Game Theory & Strategic Decision-Making**

---

## What is AIgopoly?

AIgopoly is a competitive business simulation where students run rival companies in a shared market. Outcomes depend on relative performance against competitors—making it an ideal tool for teaching game theory, strategic thinking, and financial literacy.

---

## Game Theory Focus

- **Strategic interdependence** – Your market share depends on competitors' moves, not just your own decisions
- **Nash equilibrium** – Students discover that optimal strategies emerge from anticipating rivals
- **Repeated game dynamics** – Multi-round play reveals learning, signaling, and retaliation patterns

---

## Round Structure

| Round | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| **Round 0 (Trial)** | Practice round—experiment freely, results don't count |
| **Rounds 1-5** | Scoring rounds—Round 1 resets everyone to equal starting position |

**Timing:** 15-30 min per decision phase | Full game: 2-3 hours or spread across weeks

## Six Decision Areas

| Area | Key Decisions |
|------|---------------|
| **Production** | Capacity %, supplier choice, outsourcing |
| **HR** | Hiring, wages, training level |
| **R&D** | Research hours to unlock product features |
| **Marketing** | Pricing by region, promotion spend |
| **Logistics** | Which factory serves which market |
| **Finance** | Debt/equity, dividends, buybacks |

## How Market Share Works

Market share = **Team's Attractiveness ÷ All Teams' Attractiveness**

Six factors, weighted by region:

| Factor | US | Asia | Europe |
|--------|:--:|:----:|:------:|
| Price | 22% | 40% | 18% |
| Quality | 25% | 17% | 25% |
| Features | 28% | 28% | 22% |
| Promotion | 5% | 5% | 10% |
| CSR | 15% | 5% | 25% |
| Training | 5% | 5% | 5% |

**Key insight:** Asia is price-driven, Europe values sustainability, US is balanced.

---

## How Share Price Works

```
Share Price = EPS × P/E Ratio (15) × Growth Premium
```

**Growth premium** (capped -20% to +35%) based on:
- Capacity growth (10%)
- Market share growth (10%)
- Brand strength (8%)
- Cash position (4%)

Price smoothing: 45% previous + 55% new calculation

## Dynamic Scenarios

Each round can introduce new economic conditions:

| Scenario | Effect | Strategic Response |
|----------|--------|-------------------|
| **Recession** | Demand -25%, price sensitivity ↑ | Conserve cash, cut costs |
| **Trade War** | Tariffs $10→$25/unit | Localize production |
| **Green Revolution** | CSR importance +80% | Eco-friendly suppliers, target Europe |
| **Boom** | Demand surge, costs rise | Expand aggressively |
